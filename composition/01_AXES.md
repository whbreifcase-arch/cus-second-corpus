# Composition · The Axes & Classifications

Slice 1 named an Entity's axes (`Role · Tempo · Tool · Temperament`) but not their *values*. Here they
are. Two of them (Role, Tool) build the Frame; one (Tempo) sets cadence; one (Temperament) stays
independent. Plus two classifications (Rank, Creature Type) that are not Force-axes but are real fields.

## The four axes of Force

An agent's relationship to **Force** ([Slice 1 · 01_PRIMITIVES](../slice-1/01_PRIMITIVES.md)) is read
through four **independent** axes — never collapsed into one another.

### Role — *what relationship to Force?*
The purpose the figure exists to serve.

| Role | Does what to Force |
|---|---|
| **Pressure** | **applies** Force — threatens, breaches, displaces, suppresses, pursues; forces the opposition to respond |
| **Anchor** | **prevents** Force — holds, blocks, absorbs, protects, delays, intercepts; denies hostile Force its intended change |
| **Utility** | **changes** Force — amplifies, weakens, redirects, restores, reveals, coordinates, relocates; alters the *conditions* of Force |

### Tool — *through what medium?*
How the Force is delivered. (The Combat module's controlled vocabulary.)

**Melee · Ranged · Hybrid**

### Tempo — *at what cadence?*
The **rhythm of the decision loop** — the interval between an opportunity and the figure's next
*effective application of Force.*

**Slow · Normal · Fast** (ordinal)

- **Fast** — immediate, repeatable, low preparation, low commitment; frequent repositioning or response.
- **Normal** — the loop generally opens and closes within one ordinary activation.
- **Slow** — preparation, telegraphing, commitment, payoff, or recovery spread across multiple actions or turns.

> **Tempo is *not* movement speed** (`tempo-is-not-speed`). Movement is Position — you spend Agency on <!-- retired-lint: allow tempo-speed reason: states Tempo is NOT movement speed -->
> MOVE. Tempo is the *cadence of applying Force*: artillery can out-range everything and still be Slow;
> cavalry is Fast. And Tempo is **read by humans, never by the resolver** (Law 10) — an ordinal
> classification that shapes how you *use* the figure, not a number the dice engine consumes.

### Temperament — *what behavioural bias?*
The figure's default lean when several legal applications of Force are available. **Independent of
function** — an Aggressive Gunslinger is still a Gunslinger.

**Cowardly · Resolute · Aggressive · Protective · Ravenous**

Temperament describes *preference, not capability* — it never modifies a die. It is the **fallback
psychology** the figure reverts to (e.g. leaderless, or Broken).

## Classifications (not Force-axes, but real fields)

- **Rank** — the base **shape**: **Square** (2 AP · has facing · moves in formation) or **Circle** (3 AP · faceless · never in formation). Sets Agency and geometry; not a Force-axis.
- **Creature Type** — what the figure *is*: **Man · Beast · Spirit · Construct**. Governs broad biological/morale/repair/targeting relationships a module may key on.

## Where each axis goes in composition

| Axis / field | Feeds |
|---|---|
| **Role** × **Tool** | the **Frame** (functional cell) → [02_ARCHETYPE](02_ARCHETYPE.md) |
| **Tempo** | the Archetype's **cadence** → 02_ARCHETYPE |
| **Temperament** | a **Figure Definition** field, independent of Archetype → [03_UNIT_PROFILE](03_UNIT_PROFILE.md) |
| **Rank** | Chassis (shape/Agency) |
| **Creature Type** | Chassis |
